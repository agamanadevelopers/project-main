Logo

Using the mark

Two lockups cover every background. Give the mark room to breathe and never recolour it.

Colour logo — on paper, cream, and light collateral.
White logo — on teal, photography, and dark collateral.

Do

  • Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the “a”.
  • Use the white logo on dark or busy photographic backgrounds.
  • Keep the wordmark legible — roughly 120px minimum width in print.

Don’t

  • Recolour the logo outside the brand palette.
  • Stretch, rotate, or add shadows and effects.
  • Place the colour logo on dark or low-contrast grounds.
